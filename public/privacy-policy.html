DigiSentry ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free AI-powered digital fraud protection application developed for users in India and worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how DigiSentry handles information when you use our app or website at fraudshield-omega.vercel.app.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

DigiSentry is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number

  • Any personal or sensitive data

3. How the App Works

When you use DigiSentry's AI features (Link Scanner, Message Checker, Phone Lookup):

  • Your input (link, message, or phone number) is sent to our secure AI analysis service
  • The AI processes your request and returns a result
  • The input and result are NOT stored or logged anywhere
  • No human ever sees your input
  • Each analysis is completely independent and anonymous
